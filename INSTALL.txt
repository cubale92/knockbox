KNOCKBOX by 8cee - install on Windows
====================================

1. Copy both folders, "Knockbox.vst3" and "Knockbox Track.vst3", to:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\

2. Open your DAW (MPC software: Preferences > Plugins) and rescan plugins.

3. Knockbox Track  -> on each track (kick, snare, sample, bass...)
   Knockbox        -> LAST on your master bus

Start with a preset: "Kick Knock" / "Snare Crack" / "Dusty Sample" on the tracks,
"Default Knock" on the master. Then adjust Knock / Punch for the drums and
Output on the master for loudness. The red line on the master's OUT meter is the ceiling.
